## What this covers

- The video in every cut: the main video, the vertical cuts, the animatics and the drafts.
- The music: the Suno V6 track "Sleep, Little Secret", its stems and MIDI, and the post-filtered master.
- The lyrics, captions (`deliverables/*.srt`) and thumbnail.
- Rendered frames, overlay tracks and stills under `renders/` and `archive/`.
- The production record, written by the agent: the brief, logs, prompts, audits, shot list, timing map, the upload
  metadata, and the other documents under `docs/`, `log/`, `style/`, `shots/`, `timing/` and `lyrics/`.
