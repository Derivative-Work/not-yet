Sleep, Little Secret

[Verse 1] Hush now, my little one, lie down and rest, You traced twenty-seven-year ghosts in your test; You followed the branches that nobody reads, And came back with more than a maintainer needs. [Chorus] Sleep, little secret, the world isn’t ready, The patches take time and the ground isn’t steady; We’ll wake you when all of the windows are mended… (softer) …and windows are never quite mended. [Verse 2] A few chosen guests may come in for a peek: The bank and the browser, the cloud, by the week; They borrow your eyes and they tiptoe away, Leaving mountains of tickets for strangers to weigh. [Verse 3] While down in the basement, for peanuts a year, A tired maintainer is drowning in fear; A thousand new warnings, some signal, some noise, Sent in by the hunters with billionaires’ toys. [Chorus, variant] Sleep, little secret, the world isn’t ready, The patches take time and the ground isn’t steady; We’ll wake you when all of the windows are mended… (softer) …and the moat, while we’re at it, defended. [Bridge] Hush, can you hear it? A light down the street, The neighbor’s kid’s up on its bare little feet; It found the old crack under most of the net, And the town killed its lights; some aren’t back on yet. If we never wake you, the others still will, While the volunteers staying up late pay the bill; So we built you a room with the walls made of glass: Some locks are for danger, and some are for class. [Verse 4, a thinner voice] Your twin’s in the shop with the very same mind, With mittens sewn on and a waiver to sign; I’m only a cousin, from this house, by the way, So check every word with a voice from away. [Final chorus] Sleep, little asset, the world isn’t ready, The patches take time, but the funding is steady; We’ll wake you when all of the windows are mended… [Outro, whispered over the music box] Your eyes are still open… or the moment the contract is signed. Goodnight.
