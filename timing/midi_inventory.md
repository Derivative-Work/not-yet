Rules applied by the script:
- **shift**: the track's constant offset to the physical attack is applied when events are exported (`timing/events/`).
- **ghosts** (onset in stem silence below −50 dBFS) are dropped from the exported events; the counts are in each event file.
- **stem**: a percussive stem whose MIDI explains under 30% of its detected onsets drives its machines from the stem's own onsets (`timing/stem_events/`).
- **ignore**: fragments too small or too ghostly to validate.
- Whether Suno's MIDI or a Basic Pitch re-transcription drives each instrument is decided after both are measured (`scripts/event_sources.py`, `config/event_sources.json`).
